College Algebra

  • Polynomial Explorer – allows students to add/remove and move roots of polynomials around the x-axis, showing how graphs and formulas change when number of roots increases and when roots coincide.
  • Rational Explorer – allows students to add/remove and move roots and asymptotes of rational functions, showing how graphs and formulas change as roots and asymptotes move and coincide.
  • Exponential Explorer – allows students to view exponentials using either 'base' or 'rate' notation (y=abx or y=a(1+r)x) and shows how changing multiplier and initial value will change graph and formula.
  • Compound Interest Visualizer – allows students to explore the effects of changing interest rates and compounding frequency on the formula and graph for discretely compounding growth.
